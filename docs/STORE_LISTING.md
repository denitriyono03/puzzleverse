## Long description
PuzzleVerse is a calm, offline-first puzzle platform with four original games:
Crown Grid, a queen-placement logic puzzle; Sun & Moon, a binary logic puzzle;
Path Link, a path-drawing puzzle; and Common Thread, a word-association puzzle.

Play the daily challenge, build a streak, or enjoy unlimited free play. Every
puzzle is generated and validated by the app's own rules engine, with hints,
undo, and results that respect your time. Guest play works without an account,
and your progress, history, and preferences stay on your device.

Features: daily challenges, free play, statistics and achievements, light and
dark themes, accessibility options, and a premium option that removes ads and
unlocks extra themes and insights. Core gameplay is free and fair — no
pay-to-win mechanics.
