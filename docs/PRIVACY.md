## What we collect
In the current build, PuzzleVerse stores data only on your device
(localStorage): your profile details (name, avatar choice, bio), game
history and statistics, preferences, local friends, and feedback you submit.
No account data, game history, or analytics is sent to a server by this build.

## What we do not collect
We do not sell personal data. We do not collect precise location, contacts,
photos, or data from other apps. Advertising and analytics are off by
default in this build; if a future version adds them, this policy must be
updated first and consent must be obtained in-app.

## Your rights
You can export your local data as JSON from Privacy & Account, and you can
delete your local account and data from the same screen. Deletion removes
local data from this device; it cannot delete data from a server because
this build does not send data to a server.
